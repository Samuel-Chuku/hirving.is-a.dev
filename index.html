How I work

End-to-end.
No gaps in
the pipeline.

Every system I build is designed as a complete loop — input, processing, memory, and delivery. I don't hand off half-built solutions. I own the full stack from form submission to final output.

My preferred foundation is n8n for orchestration, Airtable for structured data, Redis for session state, and OpenRouter for LLM access — giving clients a system that's powerful, maintainable, and debuggable.

Multi-Agent · Production · Lagos 2024
Vael — Samuel Automobiles AI

A full multi-agent customer support system for a Lagos automotive dealership. Orchestrator agent Vael routes to three specialized sub-agents: Kaen for knowledge & FAQs, Ryx for bookings & scheduling, Nol for escalation & ticketing. Session memory via Redis, persistent storage on Postgres, backed by Airtable, Google Sheets, Gmail, and Google Calendar.
